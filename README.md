Started working with FiveM at 18 and never really stopped. What began with small scripts turned into 5 years of full-cycle development across some of the most played servers in Brazil.

Worked on competitive game modes, battle royale systems, custom anti-cheat, server infrastructure, matchmaking, NUI interfaces and just about anything that runs inside a FiveM server. The experience covers everything from low-level game mechanics to full infrastructure design.

Lead Developer on Goat Battle Royale and Kush PvP, CTO on Boss Battle Royale and 1v99 PvP. A significant part of the mechanics, features and standards behind the Brazilian competitive FiveM scene today were first shipped on these projects.
